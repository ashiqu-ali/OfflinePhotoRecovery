# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone taking part in the project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any other place where people represent the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, identity or experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or personal and political attacks.
- Never publish anyone's private information, including data recovered from a device.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

The full text linked above is what applies; this summary doesn't replace it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at **ashiquali007@gmail.com**.
Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a
warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
